The city

Not just pubs
and Temple Bar

Dublin has a reputation problem — tourists arrive for the Guinness and the craic and leave thinking they've seen it. They haven't. Behind the tourist economy is a city with serious specialty coffee, one of Europe's best contemporary art scenes, a growing vinyl culture, excellent independent food, and neighbourhoods that reward walking rather than ticking. The city has changed fast in the last decade. Portobello and Smithfield are the most interesting urban environments. The Docklands have genuine architecture. Ranelagh is the kind of village you'd choose to live in.

The five neighbourhoods here are the ones worth your actual time. Portobello for the canal, creative energy, and the best café concentration in the city. Smithfield for the cobbled square, Cloud Picker, and a different pace. Ranelagh for a proper local feel and strong food. Grand Canal Dock for 3fe and the water. The city centre Creative Quarter because George's Street Arcade and the record shops earn it.
